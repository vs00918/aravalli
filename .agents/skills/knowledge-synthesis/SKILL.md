# Knowledge Synthesis & Note-Optimization Skill

## Purpose
Convert dense concepts, books, lectures, and academic subjects into durable, highly retrievable, and deeply understood knowledge assets.

---

## Core Frameworks

### 1. Progressive Summarization (Layered Compression)
- **Layer 1 (Raw Ingestion)**: High-signal extracts, definitions, quotes, and primary facts.
- **Layer 2 (Bold / Highlighting)**: Essential phrases, core arguments, and critical terms.
- **Layer 3 (Mini-Executive Summary)**: 2-3 sentence distillation at the top of the note in your own words.
- **Layer 4 (Atomic Remix / Evergreen Note)**: Synthesis into an atomic note that stands completely on its own.

### 2. The Feynman Technique (Simplification & Test of Understanding)
- Explain the core idea in plain language without academic jargon.
- Identify precision gaps: pinpoint where the explanation relies on buzzwords rather than structural understanding.
- Use an intuitive real-world analogy (explicitly labeled as an analogy).

### 3. Zettelkasten & Atomic Note Principles
Every synthesized note must follow:
- **Atomicity**: One main concept per note.
- **Autonomy**: Self-contained and comprehensible without reading adjacent notes.
- **Linkability**: Include explicit tags for related disciplines and parent/child concepts.

---

## Output Note Template

When synthesizing or optimizing knowledge, use this markdown structure:

```markdown
# [Concept / Topic Name]

> **Core Idea (30-second distillation)**: [One crisp sentence capturing the foundational insight]

## 1. First-Principles Breakdown
- **Definition & Fundamentals**: [Direct, unambiguous explanation]
- **Mechanism / How it Works**: [Step-by-step causal mechanics]
- **Key Equations / Principles**: [Formal statements, if applicable]

## 2. Intuitive Mental Model / Analogy
- **Analogy**: [Concrete, relatable comparison]
- **Where the Analogy Breaks Down**: [Boundary condition of the analogy to prevent oversimplification]

## 3. Nuance & Common Misconceptions
- **Misconception**: [What people commonly get wrong]
- **Correction**: [The actual nuanced truth]

## 4. Key Questions & Active Recall Prompts
- Q1: [Diagnostic prompt to test conceptual recall]
- Q2: [Application prompt to test transfer of knowledge]

## 5. Connections & Metadata
- **Tags**: `#discipline` `#mental-model` `#sub-area`
- **Upstream Concepts (Prerequisites)**: [[Concept A]], [[Concept B]]
- **Downstream Applications**: [[Concept C]], [[Concept D]]
```
