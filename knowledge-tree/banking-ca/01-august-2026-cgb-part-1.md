### 2. RBI 'On Tap' Licensing Guidelines for Urban Cooperative Banks (UCBs)

**Know & Understand (Context)**:
- **Licensing Architecture**: On-tap licensing allows qualified entities meeting capital and governance criteria to apply for UCB licenses continuously rather than waiting for ad-hoc regulatory windows.

* **Priority:** P1 — Critical / Deep | **Revision Effort:** ~8 min | **Category:** Banking Regulation & Co-operatives
* **Source:** CGB Mentors

**What Happened**
* **Cooperative Banking Transition → Commercial Bank Conversion:**
  * RBI released 'On Tap' licensing guidelines allowing eligible primary Urban Cooperative Banks (UCBs) with multi-state operations to convert into commercial banks.

**Must Memorize (Eligibility Thresholds)**
* **Conversion Thresholds Ladder:**
  * **Track Record:** Minimum **10 years** continuous operations.
  * **Deposit Base:** Minimum **₹10,000 Crore** (audited as of March 31).
  * **Net Worth:** Minimum **₹300 Crore**.
  * **CRAR & Asset Quality:** Minimum **12% CRAR** $mid$ Maximum **3% Net NPA**.
  * **Financial Trend:** Positive over previous **5 years**.
  * **Scope & Shareholding:** Multi-State Cooperative Societies Act 2002 units $mid$ Individual shareholding capped at **5%**.
  * 🎯 **Recap:** *UCB conversion: 10-yr track record $mid$ ₹10k Cr deposits $mid$ ₹300 Cr net worth $mid$ CRAR $ge 12%$, Net NPA $le 3%$.*

### 4. RBI Loan Recovery Directions & Financed Smartphone Lockout Norms

**Know & Understand (Context)**:
- **Fair Practices Code**: Prohibits lenders and recovery agents from deploying remote handset-locking malware, ensuring borrower dignity and digital privacy.

* **Priority:** P1 — Critical / Memorize | **Revision Effort:** ~6 min | **Category:** Consumer Protection & Digital Lending
* **Effective Date:** **1 January 2027** | **Source:** CGB Mentors

**What Happened**
* **Borrower Protection & Debt Recovery Safeguards:**
  * Notified directions governing recovery agents and prohibiting predatory device lockouts.

**Must Memorize**
* **Calling Norms & Smartphone Locks Ladder:**
  * **Calling Hours:** Strictly **8:00 AM to 7:00 PM** (No calls during bereavement/emergencies).
  * **Agent Certification:** Mandatory **IIBF Certification**.
  * **Device Lockout Stages:** No restriction before **30 DPD**; complete restrictions permitted only after **60 DPD**.
  * **Emergency Safeguard:** Incoming calls, SMS, and emergency SOS **can NEVER be disabled**.
  * **Restoration:** Unlock within **1 hour** of payment (Delay penalty: **₹250/hour** on bank).
  * 🎯 **Recap:** *Calling: 8 AM–7 PM $mid$ Smartphone lockout only after 60 DPD (SOS never disabled) $mid$ ₹250/hr unlock delay fine.*
