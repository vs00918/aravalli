### 1. PFRDA Digital Architecture: NPS PRIDE-Disha & Pension Sahayak Redressal
* **Priority:** P1 — Critical / Deep
* **Category:** PENSION_SYSTEMS
* **Institution:** PFRDA
* **Event Date:** 2026-07-12
* **Revision Effort:** ~7 min
* **Status:** IMPLEMENTED

#### What Happened
* **Pension Performance Benchmarking & Grievance Digitalisation:**
  * PFRDA rolled out **NPS PRIDE-Disha** and **Pension Sahayak** for NPS, UPS, and APY subscribers.
  * Standardized XIRR cashflow returns and enforced strict two-tier grievance resolution SLAs.

#### Must Memorize
* **PRIDE-Disha & Pension Sahayak Ladder:**
  * **PRIDE-Disha:** Evaluates PFM returns using **Extended Internal Rate of Return (XIRR)** methodology over actual periodic contribution cashflows (replacing point-to-point CAGR).
  * **Pension Sahayak Timelines:**
    * **Tier-1 (Intermediary - PoP/CRA):** Mandatory resolution within **30 calendar days**.
    * **Tier-2 (NPS Trust Escalation):** Binding decision within **21 calendar days**.
  * **Multilingual Support:** Powered by **Bhashini platform** across **22 Indian languages**.
  * 🎯 **Recap:** *PRIDE-Disha uses XIRR $mid$ Pension Sahayak: 30 days (PoP) $	o$ 21 days (NPS Trust) across 22 languages.*

#### Know / Understand
* Moves pension evaluation from static CAGR to actual investor cashflow returns (XIRR), and enforces binding SLAs on pension intermediaries through AI-driven escalation.

#### Exam Focus
* PRIDE-Disha methodology: Extended Internal Rate of Return (XIRR).
* Pension Sahayak resolution timelines: 30 days for Intermediaries $	o$ 21 days for NPS Trust.
* Language coverage: 22 languages via Bhashini.

### 2. SEBI Capital Market Investor Protection: Quick Transmission Processing (QTP) & Buyback ISIN Freeze
* **Priority:** P1 — Critical / Memorize
* **Category:** CAPITAL_MARKETS
* **Institution:** SEBI
* **Event Date:** 2026-07-18
* **Revision Effort:** ~7 min
* **Status:** NOTIFIED

#### What Happened
* **Securities Transmission & Insider Trading Firewalls:**
  * Streamlined inheritance transfers for deceased investors via **Quick Transmission Processing (QTP)**.
  * Mandated automated **ISIN-level freeze on promoter shareholdings** during share buybacks.

#### Must Memorize
* **QTP Limits & Buyback Safeguards Ladder:**
  * **QTP Simplified Limits:** Fast-track inheritance claims up to:
    * **Physical Securities:** Value up to **₹10,000**.
    * **Demat Holdings:** Value up to **₹30,000**.
  * **Promoter Buyback ISIN Freeze:** Promoter and promoter group holdings are **frozen at ISIN level in depositories** from Board/Shareholder approval date until offer closure.
  * **SCORES 2.0 SLA:** Listed entities must submit **Action Taken Report (ATR) within 21 days** of receiving an investor grievance.
  * 🎯 **Recap:** *QTP limits: ₹10k (Physical) / ₹30k (Demat) $mid$ Promoter shares frozen at ISIN level during buybacks $mid$ SCORES ATR in 21 days.*

#### Exam Focus
* QTP monetary limits: ₹10,000 (Physical) vs ₹30,000 (Demat).
* Promoter buyback freeze: Applies from Board/Shareholder approval date until offer closure.
* SCORES 2.0 ATR timeline: 21 days.
