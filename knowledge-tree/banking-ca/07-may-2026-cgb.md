* **Priority:** P1 — Critical / Deep

* **Institution:** RBI

* **Status:** NOTIFIED

#### What Happened
* **Record Surplus Transfer → ECF Framework Allocation:**
  * 623rd Central Board meeting held in Mumbai under Governor **Sanjay Malhotra**.
  * Approved a historic surplus dividend transfer of **₹2,86,588.46 Crore** to the Central Government for 2025-26.
  * Retained the **Contingent Risk Buffer (CRB)** at **6.5%** of balance sheet (within 4.5%–7.5% ECF band).

#### Must Memorize
* **Surplus & Risk Buffer Allocations Ladder:**
  * **Surplus Dividend Transfer:** **₹2,86,588.46 Crore** *(highest-ever central bank dividend)*
  * **Contingent Risk Buffer (CRB) Addition:** **₹1,09,379.64 Crore** transferred to CRB *(vs ₹44,861.70 Cr in FY25)*
  * **CRB Maintained Ratio:** **6.5%** of balance sheet *(target band: 4.5% to 7.5%)*
  * **Balance Sheet Expansion:** Grew by **+20.61% to ₹91,97,121.08 Crore** (~₹91.97 Lakh Cr as of March 31, 2026)
  * **Net Income:** Reached **₹3,95,972.10 Crore** *(Gross income +26.42%)*
  * 🎯 **Recap:** *RBI Dividend: ₹2,86,588.46 Cr $mid$ CRB Buffer: 6.5% (₹1,09,379.64 Cr) $mid$ Balance Sheet: ₹91.97 Lakh Cr.*

#### Know / Understand
* Robust earnings from interest on foreign/domestic assets and spot forex sales ($53.13B net sales) fortified internal buffers while providing major non-tax fiscal support to the Union Budget.

#### Exam Focus
* Exact surplus dividend amount transferred to the Government (₹2,86,588.46 crore).
* CRB percentage maintained (6.5%) and ECF prescribed range (4.5%–7.5%).
* Amount transferred to CRB (₹1,09,379.64 crore).

### 2. RBI Finalizes Revised Lending & Housing Loan Norms for Urban Co-operative Banks (UCBs)
* **Priority:** P1 — Critical / Deep
* **Category:** BANKING_REGULATION
* **Institution:** RBI
* **Event Date:** 2026-05-15
* **Revision Effort:** ~8 min
* **Status:** NOTIFIED

#### What Happened
* **Cooperative Lending Framework → Unsecured Caps & Housing Limits:**
  * RBI issued final revised lending directions for Urban Co-operative Banks (UCBs).
  * Enforces aggregate/individual unsecured exposure limits and restricts housing loan tenures and moratoria.
  * **Effective Date:** Takes effect from **October 1, 2026**.

#### Must Memorize
* **Unsecured Lending Ceilings Ladder:**
  * **Aggregate Unsecured Cap:** Capped at **20% of total loans and advances** *(shifted from earlier 10% of total assets)*.
  * **Tier-Specific Individual Ceilings:**
    * **Tier-1 UCBs:** Maximum **₹5 Lakh** per borrower
    * **Tier-2 UCBs:** Maximum **₹7.5 Lakh** per borrower
    * **Tier-3 & Tier-4 UCBs:** Maximum **₹10 Lakh** per borrower
  * **Small PSL Exemption:** Unsecured loans $le mathbf{₹50,000}$ qualifying as PSL are excluded from the 20% cap for ECBA-compliant UCBs.
* **Housing Loan Limits for Tier-1 & Tier-2 UCBs:**
  * **Maximum Tenure:** Capped at **20 years** (inclusive of moratorium).
  * **Moratorium Cap:** Permitted **only for under-construction properties up to 24 months** *(prohibited for ready-to-move purchases)*.
  * 🎯 **Recap:** *UCB unsecured cap: 20% total loans $mid$ Tier-1 (₹5L), Tier-2 (₹7.5L), Tier-3/4 (₹10L) $mid$ Housing tenure $le 20$ yrs $mid$ Moratorium $le 24$ mo.*

#### Know / Understand
* Restricts unsecured risk concentration in smaller cooperative banks while ensuring micro-credit flow and preventing maturity mismatches in urban housing loans.

#### Exam Focus
* Aggregate unsecured lending cap (20% of total loans).
* Individual unsecured caps across UCB Tiers: Tier-1 (₹5L), Tier-2 (₹7.5L), Tier-3/4 (₹10L).
* Small PSL loan exclusion threshold (₹50,000).
* Housing loan tenure cap for Tier-1/2 UCBs (20 years) and maximum construction moratorium (24 months).
* Effective date: October 1, 2026.

### 3. RBI Overhauls Foreign Exchange Authorization & Forex Correspondent (FxC) Model
* **Priority:** P1 — Critical / Deep
* **Category:** BANKING_REGULATION
* **Institution:** RBI
* **Event Date:** 2026-05-12
* **Revision Effort:** ~8 min
* **Status:** NOTIFIED

#### What Happened
* **Forex Market Liberalization → Forex Correspondent Model:**
  * Overhauled authorization framework under **FEMA (Authorized Persons) Regulations, 2026**.
  * Introduced the **Forex Correspondent (FxC)** model allowing AD-I and AD-II entities to appoint agents for retail forex operations.

#### Must Memorize
* **FxC Model & Capital Requirements Ladder:**
  * **Parent Entities:** AD Category-I and AD Category-II can appoint FxCs under principal-agent model.
  * **Permitted FxC Scope:** (1) Purchase FX notes/coins/travellers' cheques; (2) Sell FX notes/cheques for private/business travel; (3) Act as MTSS sub-agents.
  * **AD Category-II Cap:** Capped at **₹25 Lakh per foreign trade transaction**.
  * **Minimum Net Worth:** **₹10 Crore (AD-II)** $mid$ **₹2 Crore (AD-III)**.
  * **2-Year Turnover Mandate:** Must achieve and maintain annual turnover of **₹50 Crore (AD-II)** and **₹10 Crore (FFMCs)** within 2 years.
  * 🎯 **Recap:** *AD-I & AD-II can appoint FxCs $mid$ AD-II cap: ₹25L/txn $mid$ Net worth: AD-II ₹10 Cr, AD-III ₹2 Cr $mid$ Turnover: ₹50 Cr / ₹10 Cr.*

#### Know / Understand
* Expands retail forex reach across non-metro towns through agent networks while anchoring regulatory accountability to parent banks.

#### Exam Focus
* Eligible parent entities to appoint FxCs (AD-I and AD-II).
* AD Category-II transaction ceiling (₹25 lakh per trade transaction).
* Minimum net worth: ₹10 crore (AD-II) and ₹2 crore (AD-III).
* Minimum 2-year turnover thresholds: ₹50 crore (AD-II) and ₹10 crore (FFMC).

### 4. RBI Discontinues Mandatory Investment Fluctuation Reserve (IFR) for Banks
* **Priority:** P1 — Critical / Memorize
* **Category:** BANKING_REGULATION
* **Institution:** RBI
* **Event Date:** 2026-05-18
* **Revision Effort:** ~5 min
* **Status:** NOTIFIED

#### What Happened
* **Prudential Reserve Rationalization → IFR Discontinued:**
  * Discontinued mandatory creation and maintenance of **Investment Fluctuation Reserve (IFR)** by commercial banks.
  * **Effective Date:** Came into force on **May 18, 2026**.

#### Must Memorize
* **Accounting Treatment & Balance Transfers:**
  * Existing IFR balances as of May 17, 2026 transferred **'below the line' to Statutory Reserve, General Reserve, or Balance of Profit & Loss Account**.
  * **Foreign Banks in India:** Transferred directly to Indian books' statutory reserves or non-repatriable remittable surplus.
  * **SFBs and Payments Banks:** Transfers must come from net profit after mandatory statutory appropriations.
  * 🎯 **Recap:** *IFR discontinued from May 18, 2026 $mid$ Existing balances transferred below the line to Statutory/General Reserves.*

#### Know / Understand
* IFR was created to absorb yield volatility in AFS/HFT portfolios. With modern mark-to-market accounting under Basel III, standalone IFR became redundant.

#### Exam Focus
* Effective date of IFR discontinuation: May 18, 2026.
* Accounting treatment of existing IFR balances (transferred 'below the line' to Statutory/General Reserve or P&L).
* Treatment for foreign bank branches.
