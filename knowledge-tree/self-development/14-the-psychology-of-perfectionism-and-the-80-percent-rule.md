Why does a perfectionist always swing between manic overwork and depressive paralysis?

```

                │
                ▼
100% Intense Extreme Effort (High somatic strain & friction)
                │
                ▼

                │
                ▼

                │
                ▼

                │
                ▼
```

## 4. The Psychological Formula of Perfectionism

The lecture introduces an intuitive mental equation to measure procrastination pressure:

$$\mathbf{\text{Procrastination Pressure}} = \text{Fear of Judgment} \times (\text{Unrealistic Standard})^2$$

### Why Expectations are Squared $(\text{Standard})^2$:
- If you double your standard of expected perfection (from "Good Enough" to "Flawless Masterpiece"), **the psychological intimidation quadruples**.
- The prefrontal cortex becomes so overwhelmed by the fear of falling short that it completely shuts down execution and forces you to escape into social media or sleep.

### Rule 1: Adopt the Universal Axiom ($Done > Perfect$)
- A finished, imperfect essay that is submitted gets a grade. An unwritten masterpiece gets a zero.
- An imperfect video uploaded to YouTube gathers real audience data. An unrecorded video gathers dust.

### Rule 2: Unmask the Subconscious Fear
- Whenever you catch yourself saying *"I need to learn more before I start"*, stop and ask:
  > *"What am I actually afraid of? Am I afraid of people laughing at me? Am I afraid of looking inexperienced?"*
- Naming the fear strips away its illusion of "high standards."

### Rule 3: The 80% Execution Rule
- **Never wait for 100% readiness or perfect conditions.**
- The moment your idea, knowledge, or setup is **80% ready**, execute immediately.
- The remaining 20% can only be learned and perfected through the friction of real-world feedback.

### Rule 4: Focus on the 1% Process, Not the 100% Outcome
- Stop staring at the daunting mountain of the finished product.
- Commit to putting in **1% of daily effort today**: write 1 paragraph, record 30 seconds of video, read 2 pages.

---

## 6. Summary Takeaways

1. **Perfectionism is fear of judgment in disguise**, not dedication to excellence.
2. Unrealistic standards square the psychological pressure, inevitably triggering the **Burnout-Shutdown Loop**.
3. Real-world feedback on an imperfect product is worth 100x more than theoretical perfection in your imagination.
4. Apply the **80% Rule**: Launch when you are 80% ready; polish through real-world iteration.
5. Live by the master rule: **$\mathbf{Done > Perfect}$**.
