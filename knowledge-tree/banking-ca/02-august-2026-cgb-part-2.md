### 1. RBI Draft Master Directions: Interest Rates on Loans and Advances 2026
* **Priority:** P1 — Critical / Deep | **Revision Effort:** ~10 min | **Category:** Banking Regulation & Lending Framework
* **Proposed Effective Date:** **1 April 2027** | **Source:** CGB Mentors (p.6–7)

**What Happened**
* The RBI issued draft directions standardizing interest rate setting and reset mechanisms across all regulated lending entities to enhance transparency and consumer protection.

**Must Memorize (Core Rules)**
* **Benchmarking**: All loans (fixed or floating) must be linked to an internal or external benchmark plus a transparent risk-based spread. Lenders **CANNOT price loans below the applicable benchmark**.
* **Reset Frequency for Floating Loans**:
  * Chosen by the lender, but **cannot be reset more than once every 3 months**.
  * Once fixed, the reset frequency **must remain unchanged throughout the loan tenor**.
* **Agricultural Loans**: Reset period linked to crop season, capped at a maximum of **12 months**.
* **Internal Benchmark (MCLR)**: Commercial banks, RRBs, Tier 3 & Tier 4 UCBs, and rural co-operative banks with deposits $>₹1,000\text{ crore}$ will continue using **Marginal Cost of Funds-based Lending Rate (MCLR)** for internal benchmarking under a standardized calculation formula.
* **External Benchmark (EBLR)**: Mandatory external benchmark linkage retained for all floating-rate personal loans and floating-rate loans to MSMEs extended by commercial banks.
* **Interest Application**: Interest on advances must generally be charged at **monthly rests**.

**Know / Understand**
* Eliminates opaque internal benchmark practices and prevents banks from altering reset cycles to borrower disadvantage during rate hike cycles.

### 2. SEBI Reforms Mutual Fund Registration: Single Form A & Route 2 Sponsor Norms
* **Priority:** P1 — Critical / Deep | **Revision Effort:** ~7 min | **Category:** Capital Markets & Asset Management
* **Source:** CGB Mentors (p.9–10)

**What Happened**
* SEBI replaced multiple cumbersome forms (Form A in-principle + Forms C & D final) with a **Single Consolidated Form A** covering both in-principle approval and final AMC registration, while rationalizing sponsor eligibility under Regulation 5.

**Must Memorize (Sponsor Eligibility Routes)**
* **Route 1 (Profit-Track Record)**:
  * Minimum **5 years** experience in financial services.
  * Positive net worth in all previous 5 years.
  * Average net annual profit of at least **₹10 crore** during the 5-year period.
* **Route 2 (Alternative / Non-Profit Track Record)**:
  * Minimum AMC Net Worth: **₹150 crore** (to be infused by the sponsor).
  * 5-Year Lock-In: Sponsor shareholding up to at least ₹150 crore locked in for **5 years**.
  * Executive Experience: Core leadership (CEO, COO, CRO, CCO, CIO) must have **combined experience $\ge 30$ years** (with individual experience $\ge 3$ years each).
* **Private Equity / AIF Sponsors**: Must demonstrate fund management experience $\ge 5$ years and committed/drawn capital of at least **₹5,000 crore**.

### 3. SEBI Mandates Colour-Coded "Credit Risk-o-Meter" for Debt Securities
* **Priority:** P1 — Critical / Memorize | **Revision Effort:** ~6 min | **Category:** Debt Markets & Retail Protection
* **Source:** CGB Mentors (p.7–8)

**What Happened**
* SEBI proposed a mandatory standardized, colour-coded **Credit Risk-o-Meter** for all listed debt securities, required to be displayed by issuers and Online Bond Platform Providers (OBPPs).

**Must Memorize (6 Standardized Risk Tiers)**
1. `Irish Green`: Lowest credit risk
2. `Chartreuse`: Very low risk
3. `Neon Yellow`: Low risk
4. `Caramel`: Moderate risk
5. `Dark Orange`: Moderate risk of default
6. `Red`: High to very high risk of default

**Operational Safeguards**
* **Rating Agency Disclosure**: Actual rating and Credit Rating Agency (CRA) name must be visible immediately below the meter.
* **Lowest Rating Rule**: If rated by multiple CRAs, the meter must reflect the **lowest rating assigned**.
* **Unsecured Debt Alert**: Must display **bold red text** declaring the instrument is unsecured.

### 4. Government Approves 1 Billion Polymer Banknotes of ₹10 & ₹20 for Field Trials
* **Priority:** P1 — Critical / Memorize | **Revision Effort:** ~5 min | **Category:** Currency Management & RBI Act
* **Source:** CGB Mentors (p.10–11)

**Must Memorize**
* **Statutory Provision**: Approved under **Section 25 of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934** (which governs design, form, and material of banknotes).
* **Volume**: **1 billion pieces of ₹10** and **1 billion pieces of ₹20** polymer banknotes approved for field trials and regular issuance.
* **Objective**: Longer lifespan, enhanced counterfeit resistance, cleaner note policy in tropical climates.
