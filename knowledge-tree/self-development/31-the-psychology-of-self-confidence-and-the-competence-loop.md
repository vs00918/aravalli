## 3. Somatic Reframing: Nervousness vs. Excitement

The biological adrenaline symptoms for **Terror** and **Peak Excitement** are physiologically identical:

$$\text{Elevated Pulse} + \text{Shallow Breathing} + \text{Sweaty Palms} + \text{Stomach Butterflies}$$

```
                                  SOMATIC REFRAMING
                                          │
        ┌─────────────────────────────────┴─────────────────────────────────┐
        ▼                                                                   ▼
THE THREAT INTERPRETATION (Panic)                                   THE CHALLENGE INTERPRETATION (Flow)
"My heart is racing because I am going to fail and look foolish."   "My heart is racing because my body is pumping glucose
                                                                    and oxygen to deliver peak focus on stage!"
```

- When you consciously reframe physiological arousal as **Biological Readiness**, your amygdala quiets and your Prefrontal Cortex enters peak flow.

## 4. The Cognitive Evidence File Method

Your subconscious mind functions like a courtroom judge that requires **hard empirical evidence** to believe in your capability.

```
                           THE INTERNAL COURTROOM BATTLE
                                         │
        ┌────────────────────────────────┴────────────────────────────────┐
        ▼                                                                 ▼
THE IMPOSTOR'S EVIDENCE FILE                                      THE HIGH-CONFIDENCE EVIDENCE FILE
Only records awkward moments, rejections, and mistakes.           Systematically logs micro-wins, challenges endured,
Confirmation bias deletes all victories.                          and concrete proof of growth.
```

### The 3-Step Evidence Logging Protocol:
1. Keep a dedicated **Victory Journal**.
2. Every evening, write down **3 concrete, undeniable micro-victories** (e.g. spoke up in a meeting, solved a hard problem, did not react to anger).
3. When self-doubt or Impostor Syndrome strikes before a high-stakes performance, review your Evidence File to present undeniable facts to your nervous system.

## 6. The Two Fatal Confidence Killers

1. **The Perfectionism Trap**: Believing that anything less than flawless execution equals failure.
2. **Analysis Paralysis & Over-Self-Criticism**: Mentally replaying awkward moments, which conditions synthetic fear into future experiences.

1. Cultivate **Contextual Self-Efficacy** through deliberate micro-mastery.
2. Direct your **Perceptual Filter** toward objective progress rather than isolated flaws.
3. Master **Somatic Reframing**: Interpret pre-performance adrenaline as **Excitement & Biological Fuel**.
4. Maintain a daily **Cognitive Evidence File** to systematically destroy Impostor Syndrome.
5. Trust the **Competence Flywheel**: Action creates evidence, and evidence builds unshakeable confidence.
