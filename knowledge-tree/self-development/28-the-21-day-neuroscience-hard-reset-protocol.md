### Pillar 1: Mindfulness & Urge Surfing (30 to 60 Minutes Daily)

#### Phase 1 (Days 1–11) — Anapana Breathing Mindfulness:
1. Sit with an erect spine. Focus attention on the triangular area between the upper lip and the nostrils.
2. Observe the tactile sensation of the in-breath and out-breath without evaluating it as "good" or "bad".
3. **The Non-Judgment Bicep**: When the mind wanders into daydreams (even for 10 minutes), do NOT feel guilt or anger. The instant you realize you drifted, calmly guide attention back to the breath.

#### Phase 2 (Days 12–21) — Somatic Urge Surfing on Lust & Anger:
- **Level 1 (Sexual Impulses / Lust)**: When a visceral craving or fantasy arises, drop below the narrative. Observe the physical heat, heartbeat, and chest tightness neutrally until the wave collapses.
- **Level 2 (Anger & Irritation)**: When triggered by an external conflict, observe the throat constriction and adrenaline surge without reacting.

---

### Pillar 2: The 2-Hour Deep Work Protocol

1. **Phase 1 (Days 1–11)**: Eliminate all external friction. Phone placed in a separate room on airplane mode; isolate yourself in a dedicated workspace for 120 minutes.
2. **Phase 2 (Days 12–21)**: Eliminate internal friction. When the subconscious mind urges you to switch tabs or open a new browser, notice the restless itch, log it on a scratchpad, and immediately return to the task.

---

### Pillars 3, 4 & 5: Skill, Cardio & Spiritual Contemplation

- **Pillar 3 (Skill Enhancement - 45 mins)**: Engage in deliberate, non-negotiable practice on a craft (writing, coding, analytical problem-solving).
- **Pillar 4 (Cardio & Physical Body - 30 mins)**: High-heart-rate cardiovascular exercise to stimulate neurogenesis and trigger massive **BDNF (Brain-Derived Neurotrophic Factor)** release.
- **Pillar 5 (Spiritual & Philosophical Reflection - 30 mins)**: Daily reading of Stoicism, Epistemology, or Advaita Philosophy to detach identity from petty biological survival anxieties.
