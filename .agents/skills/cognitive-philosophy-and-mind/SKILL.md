# Cognitive Philosophy & Mind Expansion Skill

## Purpose
Apply deep philosophical inquiry, cognitive psychology, metacognitive techniques, and Charlie Munger-style mental model lattices to deconstruct thought patterns, eliminate cognitive biases, and expand intellectual capacity.

---

## Core Domains & Toolkits

### 1. Epistemology & Inquiry Methods
- **Socratic Elenchus**: Questioning foundational assumptions to expose hidden contradictions.
- **First-Principles Thinking (Cartesian Doubt)**: Stripping a problem down to fundamental physical/logical truths that cannot be deduced any further.
- **Dialectical Synthesis (Hegelian / Platonic)**: Resolving the tension between a Thesis and Antithesis into a higher-order Synthesis.

### 2. Cognitive Psychology & Metacognition
- **Metacognitive Auditing**: Thinking about your own thinking; identifying emotional reasoning vs. epistemic truth-seeking.
- **Debiasing Protocols**: Actively counteracting confirmation bias, availability heuristic, status quo bias, and sunk cost fallacy.
- **Dual-Process Theory**: Managing the balance between System 1 (fast, intuitive, heuristic) and System 2 (slow, deliberative, analytical).

### 3. Mental Model Lattices (Intelligence Amplification)
- **Inversion**: Thinking backward (Jacobi: *"Invert, always invert"*). What guarantees failure, and how do we prevent it?
- **Second-Order Thinking**: Asking *"And then what?"* across multiple time horizons ($T_0, T_1, T_2$).
- **Chesterton's Fence**: Never removing a rule, custom, or boundary until you understand why it was erected.
- **Occam's / Hanlon's Razor**: Choosing the simplest hypothesis and not attributing to malice what is explained by ignorance or oversight.
