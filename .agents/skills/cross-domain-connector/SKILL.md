# Cross-Domain Connector Skill

## Purpose
Facilitate multi-disciplinary synthesis by identifying structural invariants (isomorphisms) across distinct fields, enabling lateral thinking, combinatorial creativity, and higher-order mental models.

---

## Core Methodologies

### 1. Structural Isomorphism Mapping
Identify patterns where different domains share the exact underlying mathematical, behavioral, or relational architecture:
- **Entropy**: Thermodynamic Entropy (Physics) $\leftrightarrow$ Shannon Information Entropy (Info Theory) $\leftrightarrow$ Psychological Cognitive Load.
- **Evolutionary Selection**: Natural Selection (Biology) $\leftrightarrow$ Memetics (Culture) $\leftrightarrow$ Reinforcement Learning (AI) $\leftrightarrow$ Market Competition (Economics).
- **Feedback Loops**: Homeostasis (Physiology) $\leftrightarrow$ Cybernetic Control Loops (Engineering) $\leftrightarrow$ Dynamic Equilibrium in Ecosystems (Ecology).

### 2. The 3-Step Cross-Domain Transfer Protocol
1. **De-contextualization**: Strip domain-specific jargon from Concept A to reveal its abstract functional mechanism.
2. **Isomorphism Scan**: Search target domains (e.g. History, Computer Science, Neuroscience, Philosophy) for systems that exhibit the identical functional mechanism.
3. **Re-contextualization & Synthesis**: Map the mechanics back to the target field, generating new hypotheses, problem-solving angles, or creative interpretations.
