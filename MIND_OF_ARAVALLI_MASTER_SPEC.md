---

# 1. CORE PURPOSE & PRINCIPLES
Mind of Aravalli is:
> **A living personal encyclopedia that turns scattered information into structured, connected, durable understanding.**

The system prioritizes:
1. Understanding over summarization.
2. Concepts over documents.
3. Connections over isolated notes.
4. Long-term knowledge over short-term consumption.
5. Clarity over complexity.
6. Human-readable explanations over AI jargon.
7. Traceability over unsupported AI claims.
8. Sustainable learning over information overload.

---

# 2. INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE
1. **Home**: Intellectual dashboard (*Continue Reading, Master Library, Recent Concepts, Connections, Questions to Explore*).
2. **Master Library**: Consolidated Master Chapters (*Universe & Physics, Energy & Technology, Biology & Life, Complex Systems & Human Body, Society, Money & Mind*).
3. **Concepts**: 6-level layered explanations (*Level 1 One-liner → Level 2 Intuition → Level 3 Mechanics → Level 4 First principles → Level 5 Mathematical model → Level 6 Deeper connections*).
4. **Sources**: First-class objects with full metadata (video, podcast, book, paper, article) providing complete traceability.
5. **Connections**: Explicit cross-domain relationship types (*depends_on, causes, enables, analogous_to, mathematically_related_to, emerges_from*).

# 3. BUILD SEQUENCE
- **Phase 0**: Project Foundation & Architecture (Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind, Prisma SQLite, Clean Layout).
- **Phase 1**: Visual Foundation & Design System (Navigation, Typography, Colors, Layout Shell).
- **Phase 2**: Master Library (Chapter listing, Table of Contents, Detail views).
- **Phase 3**: Concepts System (6-Layer explanation controller, Concept pages, Navigation).
- **Phase 4**: Editing & CRUD (Create/Edit chapters and concepts, attach sources).
- **Phase 5**: Global Search (Fast multi-attribute search across concepts, chapters, sources).
- **Phase 6**: Explicit Cross-Domain Relationships (Connections model & lattice).
- **Phase 7**: AI-Assisted Ingestion & Review (URL extraction, noise removal, human-in-the-loop review).
