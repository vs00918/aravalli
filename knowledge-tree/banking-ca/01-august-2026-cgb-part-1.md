### 2. RBI 'On Tap' Licensing Guidelines for Urban Cooperative Banks (UCBs)
* **Priority:** P1 — Critical / Deep | **Revision Effort:** ~8 min | **Category:** Banking Regulation & Co-operatives

**What Happened**
* The RBI issued draft guidelines allowing large credit cooperative societies to convert into full-fledged Urban Cooperative Banks (UCBs) on an ongoing ('on tap') basis.

**Must Memorize (Eligibility Thresholds)**
* **Operating Track Record**: Minimum **10 years** of continuous operations.
* **Deposit Base**: Minimum **₹10,000 crore** (audited as of March 31 of previous FY).
* **Net Worth**: Minimum **₹300 crore**.
* **Capital Adequacy (CRAR)**: Minimum **12%**.
* **Asset Quality (Net NPA)**: Maximum **3%**.
* **Financial Trend**: Positive, progressive trend over the previous **5 years**.
* **Initial Phase Scope**: Restricted to societies registered under the **Multi-State Cooperative Societies Act, 2002** with multi-state operations.
* **Governance Norm**: Individual shareholding capped at **5%**.

**Know / Understand**
* Large credit societies hold massive public deposits. Bringing them into the UCB regulatory fold subjects them to RBI CRAR standards, supervisory oversight, and DICGC deposit insurance.

**Exam Angle**
* High-probability 5-option MCQ testing exact numerical thresholds (10 yrs, ₹10k cr deposits, ₹300 cr net worth, 12% CRAR, 3% NNPA, 5% shareholding cap).

### 3. RBI Scale-Based Regulation: NBFC Upper Layer (NBFC-UL) List 2026–27
* **Priority:** P1 — Critical / Deep | **Revision Effort:** ~7 min | **Category:** NBFC Regulation

**What Happened**
* The RBI classified **Tata Sons Private Limited** as an **Upper-Layer NBFC (NBFC-UL)** under its principle-based identification framework (standalone assets $>₹2$ trillion).

**Must Memorize**
* **Total Upper Layer NBFCs**: **17 entities** identified for 2026–27.
* **Government Entities in NBFC-UL**: REC Ltd, Power Finance Corporation (PFC), Indian Railway Finance Corporation (IRFC), HUDCO.
* **5-Year Lock-In Rule**: Once classified as NBFC-UL, enhanced regulatory requirements apply for **at least 5 years**, even if the entity drops below asset/scoring thresholds in subsequent years (e.g. PNB Housing Finance, Sammaan Capital).
* **Mandatory Listing Window**: Upper Layer NBFCs must mandatorily list on stock exchanges within **3 years** of classification.

**Know / Understand (SBR Architecture)**
* Base Layer (non-deposit $<₹1,000$ cr) $\to$ Middle Layer ($\ge ₹1,000$ cr) $\to$ Upper Layer ($\ge ₹1$ lakh cr / top 10 scoring, bank-like regulation) $\to$ Top Layer (discretionary).

### 4. RBI Loan Recovery Directions & Financed Smartphone Lockout Norms
* **Priority:** P1 — Critical / Memorize | **Revision Effort:** ~6 min | **Category:** Consumer Protection & Digital Lending
* **Effective Date:** **1 January 2027** (Extended from 1 October 2026)

**Must Memorize (Operational Rules)**
* **Calling / Visiting Hours**: Strictly between **8:00 AM and 7:00 PM**. Contact during bereavement or medical emergency is prohibited.
* **Mandatory Certification**: All recovery agents must obtain certification from the **Indian Institute of Banking and Finance (IIBF)**.
* **Financed Smartphone Restrictions**:
  * No restriction can be activated until the account is **30 Days Past Due (DPD)** despite notices.
  * Complete restrictions permitted under loan agreement only after **60 DPD**.
  * **Protected Functions**: Banks can **NEVER** disable incoming calls, SMS services, or emergency SOS calling.
  * **Restoration**: Device must be unlocked within **1 hour** of payment.
  * **Bank Delay Penalty**: **₹250 per hour** of delay, capped at total loan amount.

* **Priority:** P1 — Critical / Memorize | **Revision Effort:** ~5 min | **Category:** Capital Adequacy & Basel III

**Must Memorize**
* **Domestic Systemically Important Banks (D-SIBs) Additional CET1 Surcharges**:
  * **State Bank of India (SBI)**: Bucket 4 $\to$ **0.80%** CET1 surcharge.
  * **HDFC Bank**: Bucket 2 $\to$ **0.40%** CET1 surcharge.
  * **ICICI Bank**: Bucket 1 $\to$ **0.20%** CET1 surcharge.
* **Minimum Leverage Ratio**: Unchanged at **4.0% for D-SIBs** and **3.5% for all other banks**.
* **G-SIB Branches in India**: Must maintain 3.5% + applicable G-SIB leverage buffer.
* **Derivatives Exposure Multiplier**: Calculated as $1.4 \times (\text{Replacement Cost} + \text{Potential Future Exposure})$.

### 6. RBI Credit Valuation Adjustment (CVA) Risk Capital Framework
* **Priority:** P1 — Critical / Memorize | **Revision Effort:** ~5 min | **Category:** Derivatives & Risk Management
* **Proposed Effective Date:** **1 April 2027** (Replaces 2011 norms)

**Must Memorize**
* Applies to all commercial banks except SFBs, Payments Banks, and LABs.
* **Basic Approach (BA-CVA)**: Full BA-CVA (recognizes hedges) and Reduced BA-CVA (no hedges).
* **Simplified Approach Threshold**: Available for banks with non-centrally cleared derivatives up to **₹10 trillion** (CVA capital $= 100\%$ of counterparty credit risk capital requirement).
* **Risk Weights**: Sovereigns $= 0.5\%$ (Investment Grade) / $2\%$ (others); Financial Sector $= 5\%$ (IG) / $12\%$ (others).
