## 4. Daily Training: Breathing Mindfulness as Anchor

1. **Daily 10-Minute Breath Anchor**: Sit upright and observe the subtle sensation of air passing over the nostrils without judgment.
2. **Urge Lab**: When an itch or restless thought arises during meditation, do NOT scratch or react. Observe the rising sensation until it dissolves on its own.
3. **Automated Reflex**: With consistent practice, whenever an addictive trigger hits during real life, your Prefrontal Cortex automatically enters the Witness State, neutralizing cravings effortlessly.

## 5. Summary Takeaways

1. Cravings are **biological waves** that peak and naturally dissolve within 3–5 minutes.
2. **Indulgence** feeds the habit loop; **Suppression** creates explosive rebound pressure (Pink Elephant Paradox).
3. Master cravings through **Somatic Urge Surfing** — locate the physical tension in the body and ride it without judgment.
4. Rewire addiction pathways via **Synaptic Extinction** by letting urges crest without rewarding them.
5. Build the muscle daily through **10 minutes of non-judgmental breathing mindfulness**.
