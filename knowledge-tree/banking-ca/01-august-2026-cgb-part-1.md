### 2. RBI 'On Tap' Licensing Guidelines for Urban Cooperative Banks (UCBs)
* **Priority:** P1 — Critical / Deep | **Revision Effort:** ~8 min | **Category:** Banking Regulation & Co-operatives
* **Source:** CGB Mentors

**Must Memorize (Eligibility Thresholds)**
* **Operating Track Record**: Minimum **10 years** of continuous operations.
* **Deposit Base**: Minimum **₹10,000 crore** (audited as of March 31 of previous FY).
* **Net Worth**: Minimum **₹300 crore**.
* **Capital Adequacy (CRAR)**: Minimum **12%**.
* **Asset Quality (Net NPA)**: Maximum **3%**.
* **Financial Trend**: Positive, progressive trend over the previous **5 years**.
* **Initial Phase Scope**: Restricted to societies registered under the **Multi-State Cooperative Societies Act, 2002** with multi-state operations.
* **Governance Norm**: Individual shareholding capped at **5%**.

### 3. RBI Defers Basel III Pillar 3 Disclosures to April 1, 2027 (ECL Alignment)
* **Priority:** P1 — Critical / Memorize | **Revision Effort:** ~7 min | **Category:** Banking Regulation & Basel III
* **Source:** Smartkeeda (Aug 2026) | **Origin:** RBI Notification

**What Happened**
* The RBI deferred the mandatory implementation of the revised **Basel III Pillar 3 Market Discipline Disclosures** from September 30, 2026 to **April 1, 2027**.
* Issued **10 amendment directions** revising instructions for Commercial Banks, Small Finance Banks (SFBs), and Payments Banks (PBs).

**Must Memorize**
* **Effective Implementation Date**: **April 1, 2027** (First revised quarterly disclosure applicable for quarter ending **June 30, 2027**).
* **Core Alignment**: Aligns market disclosure frameworks directly with the **Expected Credit Loss (ECL)** provisioning framework and revised credit-risk capital rules.
* **Reporting Architecture**: Applies at the **top consolidated level** of banking groups (non-top group entities provide standalone Pillar 3 disclosures).
* **Board Mandate**: Banks must maintain a formal disclosure policy approved directly by their Board of Directors.

### 4. DFS Launches India's 1st Sovereign-Backed 'Bharat Maritime Insurance Pool (BMIP)'
* **Priority:** P1 — Critical / Deep | **Revision Effort:** ~8 min | **Category:** Insurance & Trade Infrastructure
* **Source:** Smartkeeda (Aug 2026) | **Issuer:** Department of Financial Services (MoF)

**What Happened & Key Facts**
* DFS launched India’s 1st sovereign-backed **Protection & Indemnity (P&I)** insurance product under the **Bharat Maritime Insurance Pool (BMIP)**, designed by **The New India Assurance Company Limited**.
* **First Policy Issued**: Handed over to the **Shipping Corporation of India (SCI) Limited**.
* **Indemnity Capacity**: Up to **$1.5 billion** available through the combined underwriting capacity of the pool with a 24×7 global port correspondent network.
* **Strategic Objective**: Established to ensure uninterrupted maritime war-risk insurance capacity during geopolitical conflicts and eliminate reliance on foreign P&I clubs (reduced war-risk premium rates by **35–40%**; 1,608 policies issued by July 2026).

* **Priority:** P1 — Critical / Memorize | **Revision Effort:** ~5 min | **Category:** Derivatives & Risk Management
* **Effective Date:** **1 April 2027** | **Source:** CGB Mentors

**Must Memorize**
* Basic Approach (BA-CVA: Full & Reduced).
* **Simplified Approach**: For banks with non-centrally cleared derivatives $\le ₹10\text{ trillion}$ (CVA capital $= 100\%$ of CCR capital).
* **Risk Weights**: Sovereigns $= 0.5\%$ (IG) / $2\%$ (others); Financial Sector $= 5\%$ (IG) / $12\%$ (others).
