## 2. Core Mission & Philosophy
*Mind of Aravalli* is a **Living Personal Encyclopedia** that transforms scattered, high-signal information (multi-hour podcasts, philosophy, cognitive science, physics, economics, and books) into structured, deeply connected, durable understanding.

- **The Core Optimization Invariant**:
  > **Maximize insight density, conceptual cross-linking, and mental model compounding per minute of study, NOT the volume of superficial notes.**
- **Key Principles**:
  1. **Concepts over Documents**: Facts are connected into living conceptual nodes, not isolated note files.
  2. **First-Principles Understanding**: Jargon must be broken down to fundamentals before advanced synthesis.
  3. **Rigorous Canonicalization**: 10 sources discussing one core phenomenon = **1 Canonical Topic Node**. Never create duplicate entries.
  4. **Active Recall & Behavioral Integration**: Knowledge is useless unless it can be retrieved under pressure and applied in life.

## 3. The 4-Tier Cognitive Architecture (Mandatory for Canonical Nodes)

Every canonical topic in *Mind of Aravalli* follows the 4-Tier standard:
1. **Tier 1: Jargon-Free Reality Bridge**
   - Plain-English hook, everyday intuitive analogy, and immediate real-world stakes.
2. **Tier 2: Cognitive Science, Epistemic Architecture & Mechanistic Depth**
   - Detailed biological, economic, mathematical, or epistemic mechanisms, supported by Mermaid.js architectural diagrams.
3. **Tier 3: The Skeptic's Razor & Failure Mode Guardrails**
   - Common misconceptions, boundaries of applicability, edge cases, and psychological traps.
4. **Tier 4: The 24-Hour Behavioral Protocol ("The Homework")**
   - Actionable, concrete, measurable micro-protocols that can be executed immediately.

## 4. Mandatory Protocol for YouTube Video Ingestion (The 4-Pillar Pipeline)

Whenever ANY YouTube video URL is provided by the user, the agent must strictly execute this 4-Pillar Protocol:

1. **Pillar 1: Comprehensive, Self-Contained Notes (Watch-Elimination Standard)**
   - Deconstruct the video into thorough, high-resolution notes capturing the full logical arc, narrative krucibles, expert claims, nuances, and timestamped examples.
   - The depth must completely eliminate any necessity for the user to watch the original video.
2. **Pillar 2: Epistemic Verification, Fact-Checking & Myth-Debunking**
   - Cross-examine the speaker's claims against verified empirical literature, historical consensus, and epistemic razors.
   - Call out cherry-picked data, pseudoscientific leaps, cognitive distortions, or marketing exaggerations.
   - Present the verified reality with authoritative citations and grounded truth.
3. **Pillar 3: Canonical Mapping & Strict User Approval Gate**
   - Detail exactly where the knowledge fits within the Living Codex architecture:
     - Volume number, volume title, target topic slug, or proposal for a new canonical node.
     - Planned structural integration (sections to enrich or create).
   - **MANDATORY APPROVAL GATE**: The agent MUST NOT modify codebase files, build static web assets, or commit/push to git until the user reviews the placement and explicitly provides approval.
4. **Pillar 4: Ruthless Intent & Value Audit**
   - Deliver a blunt, unvarnished diagnostic audit:
     - **Core Value Delivered**: Does this expand human agency, or is it recycled conventional wisdom?
     - **Creator Intent**: Diagnosing the motive (e.g. genuine pedagogy, algorithmic retention/clickbait, personal branding, course/affiliate funnel).
     - **The Verdict**: Clear recommendation: **Real High-Signal Knowledge** vs. **Surface Fluff / Better Skipped**.
