Monetary Policy Statement, 2026-27: Resolution of the Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) August 3-5, 2026

August 05, 2026

On the basis of an assessment of the current and evolving macroeconomic situation, the Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) at its meeting today (August 5, 2026) decided to:

  • Keep the policy repo rate under the liquidity adjustment facility (LAF) unchanged at 5.25 per cent.
  • Consequently, the standing deposit facility (SDF) rate remains unchanged at 5.00 per cent and the marginal standing facility (MSF) rate and the Bank Rate at 5.50 per cent.
  • The MPC also decided to continue with the neutral stance.

The meeting was chaired by RBI Governor Sanjay Malhotra with all external and internal members present.
