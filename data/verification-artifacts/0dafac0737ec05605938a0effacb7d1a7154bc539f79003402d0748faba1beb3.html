Cabinet approves GOBARdhan (Galvanizing Organic Bio-Agro Resources Dhan) National Circular Bioenergy Scheme with outlay of Rs 23,731 crore

06 AUG 2026 6:45PM by PIB Delhi

The Union Cabinet, chaired by Prime Minister Shri Narendra Modi, has approved GOBARdhan (Galvanizing Organic Bio-Agro Resources Dhan) as the National Circular Bioenergy Scheme with a total financial outlay of Rs 23,731 crore for the period FY 2026-27 to FY 2035-36.

The scheme establishes an administered CBG purchase price of Rs 2,110 per MMBTU for 10 years, capital assistance of up to Rs 2 crore per ton per day of capacity, and an integrated framework for mandatory blending in CGD networks.
