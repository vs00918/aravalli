# Book Distiller & Analyzer Skill

## Purpose
Extract the highest-density insights from books, treatises, and long-form literature. Filter out narrative fluff and reconstruct the author's core thesis, logical proof, and practical utility using classical analytical reading principles.

---

## Analytical Reading Framework (Adler's Levels)

### 1. Classification & Scope
- What kind of book is this (theoretical, practical, historical, philosophical)?
- What is the fundamental problem or question the author is attempting to solve?

### 2. Argument Reconstruction (Terms, Propositions, Arguments)
- **Key Terms**: Unpack the author's specialized vocabulary and definitions.
- **Core Propositions**: The central assertions the author makes.
- **Argument Chain**: The deductive or inductive logic supporting those assertions.

### 3. Critical Assessment & Syntopical Linkage
- Is the author's argument sound, partially flawed, or incomplete?
- How does this book converse with competing books on the same subject?

---
