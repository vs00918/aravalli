### 1. RBI Increases Borrowing Limits Against Securities: ₹1 Crore for Shares & ₹25 Lakh for IPOs
* **Priority:** P1 — Critical / Deep
* **Category:** BANKING_REGULATION
* **Institution:** RBI
* **Event Date:** 2026-04-03
* **Revision Effort:** ~8 min
* **Status:** NOTIFIED

#### What Happened
* **Regulatory Revision → Capital Market Exposure Architecture:**
  * **Objective:** Substantially enhance individual borrowing ceilings for capital market participation while establishing macroprudential systemic safeguards.
  * **Implementation Timeline:** Deferred to **July 1, 2026** (to allow operational transition across core banking systems).

#### Must Memorize
* **Borrowing Ceilings Revision → System-Wide Risk Architecture:**
  * **Loans Against Shares / REITs / InvITs:** Raised from ₹20 lakh $	o$ **₹1 Crore per borrower**
  * **IPO / FPO / ESOP Subscriptions:** Raised from ₹10 lakh $	o$ **₹25 Lakh per individual**
  * **Scope of Limits:** Converted to **aggregate system-wide cap across all banks** *(prevents multi-bank limit circumvention)*
  * **Listed Debt Securities:** Regulatory ceiling on bank lending **completely removed**
  * **Mandatory Margins:** **50%** (Physical shares) $mid$ **25%** (Demat shares)
  * **Timeline:** Effective **July 1, 2026**
  * 🎯 **Recap:** *Shares ₹1 Cr $mid$ IPO ₹25L $mid$ Margins 50%/25% $mid$ Effective July 1, 2026.*

#### Know / Understand
* **Systemic Risk Mitigation → Single-Borrower PAN Tracking:**
  * Previously, retail investors could circumvent exposure caps by borrowing ₹20 lakh each from multiple banks simultaneously.
  * The PAN-linked system-wide ceiling of ₹1 crore (and ₹25 lakh for IPOs) provides liquidity while preventing overleveraged speculative bubbles.

#### Exam Focus
* Loans against shares ceiling: **₹1 crore per borrower** (earlier ₹20 lakh).
* IPO/FPO/ESOP financing limit: **₹25 lakh per individual** (earlier ₹10 lakh).
* System-wide aggregate cap rule across entire banking sector.
* Margin floors: **50% for physical shares** and **25% for demat shares**.
* Implementation effective date: **July 1, 2026**.
