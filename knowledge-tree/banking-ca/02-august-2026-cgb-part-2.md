### 1. RBI Draft Master Directions: Interest Rates on Loans and Advances 2026
* **Priority:** P1 — Critical / Deep | **Revision Effort:** ~8 min | **Category:** Banking Regulation & Lending Framework
* **Regulatory Status:** **DRAFT / CONSULTATION** | **Proposed Effective Date:** **1 April 2027**
* **Verification Status:** Source-only (CGB Mentors p.6–7)

**What Happened**
* The RBI issued draft directions standardizing interest rate setting and reset mechanisms across all regulated lending entities to enhance transparency and borrower protection.

**Must Memorize (Core Rules)**
* **Benchmark Pricing Rule**: All loans (fixed or floating) must be referenced to an internal or external benchmark plus a transparent risk-based spread. Lenders **CANNOT price loans below the applicable benchmark**.
* **Reset Frequency for Floating Loans**:
  * Chosen by the lender, but **cannot be reset more than once every 3 months**.
  * Once fixed, the reset frequency **must remain unchanged throughout the loan tenor**.
* **Agricultural Loans**: Reset period linked to crop season, capped at a maximum of **12 months**.
* **Internal Benchmark (MCLR)**: Commercial banks, RRBs, Tier 3 & Tier 4 UCBs, and rural co-operative banks with deposits $>₹1,000\text{ crore}$ will continue using **Marginal Cost of Funds-based Lending Rate (MCLR)** for internal benchmarking.
* **External Benchmark (EBLR)**: Mandatory external benchmark linkage retained for all floating-rate personal loans and floating-rate loans to MSMEs extended by commercial banks.
* **Interest Calculation**: Interest on advances must generally be charged at **monthly rests**.

**Know / Understand**
* Prevents banks from unilaterally altering reset dates or resetting floating rates unfairly during interest rate cycles, while preserving MCLR for large rural/cooperative lenders.
