Ministry of Finance - Department of Revenue (CBDT)

Foreign Assets of Small Taxpayers – Disclosure Scheme (FAST-DS) Rules, 2026

Notification No. 78/2026 | August 15, 2026

In exercise of powers conferred under Chapter IV (Sections 130 to 144) of the Finance Act, 2026, the Central Government hereby notifies the FAST-DS Rules, 2026.

The scheme commences on August 16, 2026 and declarations may be filed on or before December 31, 2026. Valuation date is March 31, 2026. Covers Category I (undisclosed foreign assets up to ₹1 crore) and Category II (undeclared foreign assets up to ₹5 crore). Valid declaration confers immunity from penalty and prosecution under the Black Money Act, 2015.
