Securities and Exchange Board of India

Consultation paper on the mandatory adoption of a Credit Risk-o-Meter as an additional disclosure mechanism for debt securities

August 13, 2026

SEBI proposes introducing a standardized 6-level colour-coded Credit Risk-o-Meter for all listed and proposed-to-be-listed debt securities. The 6 colour tiers map credit ratings from Irish Green (AAA, lowest credit risk) through to Red (D, default or highest credit risk).

The indicator shall be mandatorily depicted across all offer documents, abridged prospectuses, private placement memorandums (PPMs), advertisements, and online bond platform provider (OBPP) interfaces.
