Your prefrontal cortex possesses a fixed daily reserve of **Cognitive RAM (Attention & Processing Power)**. It *must* solve problems:

```
[Healthy Cognitive Challenges] ◄── Feeding Productive Food ──► [Deep Creative Flow]
                                      ▲
                                      │ (Daily Cognitive RAM)
                                      ▼
[Past Regret Loops] ◄──────── Unused Mental Energy ────────► [Future Catastrophe Dread]
```

- If you don't give your brain productive problems (reading, writing, learning a skill, building a project), **it begins to cannibalize itself**.
- It burns its cognitive RAM on:
  1. **Past Regret Loops**: Replaying past embarrassments and mistakes.
  2. **Future Anxiety**: Inventing worst-case scenarios.
  3. **Toxic Self-Criticism**: Hyper-analyzing every personal flaw.

## 7. Summary Takeaways

1. **Unspent energy always turns toxic**: Physical stagnation creates anxiety; mental stagnation creates overthinking.
2. Neutralize cortisol daily through **physical movement and exercise**.
3. Feed your cognitive RAM **deep, engaging problems** so it doesn't feed on regrets and anxieties.
4. Channel emotional energy through **the Somatic Gap, journaling, and creative sublimation**.
5. Anchor your life to an **actionable, service-driven Purpose** — the ultimate lens that blurs out life's petty noise.
