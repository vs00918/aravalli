### 2. Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023: Statutory Penalties & Regulatory Framework
* **Priority:** P1 — Critical / Memorize
* **Category:** BANKING_REGULATION
* **Institution:** Ministry of Electronics & IT (MeitY) / DPBI
* **Event Date:** 2026-08-04
* **Revision Effort:** ~7 min
* **Status:** NOTIFIED

#### What Happened
* **Data Privacy Governance & Statutory Penalties:**
  * Enforcement guidelines under DPDPA 2023 establishing statutory compliance standards and high-quantum monetary penalties for data fiduciaries.

#### Must Memorize
* **DPDPA Statutory Penalty Ladder:**
  * **Personal Data Breach (Security Failure):** Penalty up to **₹250 Crore**.
  * **Failure to Notify DPBI / Affected Individuals:** Penalty up to **₹200 Crore**.
  * **Children's Data Duty Violations:** Penalty up to **₹200 Crore**.
  * **Residual / General Non-Compliance:** Penalty up to **₹50 Crore**.
  * 🎯 **Recap:** *DPDPA Penalties: ₹250 Cr (Breach failure) $mid$ ₹200 Cr (Notification failure & Children data) $mid$ ₹50 Cr (Residual).*

#### Know / Understand
* Imposes massive balance-sheet accountability on banks and fintechs handling customer financial and identity data, making cybersecurity safeguards a top board-level governance requirement.

#### Exam Focus
* Maximum penalty for failure to prevent personal data breach: ₹250 crore.
* Maximum penalty for failure to notify data breach: ₹200 crore.
* Maximum penalty for child data violation: ₹200 crore.
