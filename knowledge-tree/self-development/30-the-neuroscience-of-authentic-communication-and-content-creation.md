## 4. Voice, Delivery & Audio Architecture

- **Pacing Over Speed**: Rapid speech overstimulates working memory; unhurried, grounded cadences allow listeners to integrate deep cognitive models.
- **Vocal Resonance**: Clean, noise-free audio (shotgun mic + AI room dereverberation) provides authority and comfort, enabling 30-to-50-minute watch times.
- **Anti-Hype Thumbnails**: Clean, clutter-free board snapshots without sensationalist faces or clickbait arrows signal high academic integrity.
