# Physical Regimes Across Scales: From Viscous Air to Gravitational Rupture

> **System Invariant**: There is no universal "objective" physical reality experienced identically by all organisms. Life spans seven orders of magnitude ($10^{-6}\text{ m} \to 10^1\text{ m}$), and each scale is dominated by entirely different governing forces: **Viscosity at the micro-scale**, **Surface Tension at the meso-scale**, and **Inertial Gravity at the macro-scale**.

## 2. Why Giant Insects Cannot Exist

* **Exoskeleton Weight Scaling**: An insect scaled to the size of a horse ($L \times 100$) would see its mass increase by $1,000,000\times$, requiring an exoskeleton so thick that its interior muscle volume would be crushed under its own structural weight.
* **Passive Tracheal Diffusion Limits**: Insects breathe via passive spiracle diffusion. Oxygen diffusion rates drop with distance squared ($t \propto x^2$), making oxygenation impossible for thick tissues without active closed circulatory systems.
