### 1. EXIM Bank Replaces RBI as Implementing Agency for Export Credit Interest Subvention
* **Priority:** P1 — Critical / Deep | **Revision Effort:** ~8 min | **Category:** Banking & Regulation / Export Finance
* **Source:** Smartkeeda (Aug 2026) | **Origin:** Ministry of Commerce & Industry / RBI Notification

**What Happened**
* The Ministry of Commerce and Industry designated the **Export-Import (EXIM) Bank of India** as the primary implementing agency for the *Interest Subvention Support Scheme on Pre- and Post-Shipment Export Credit* under the **Export Promotion Mission (EPM)**, replacing the Reserve Bank of India (RBI), which had operated the scheme on a pilot basis.

**Must Memorize (Transition & Institutional Rules)**
* **Implementing Agency**: **EXIM Bank of India** manages operational execution, online portal administration, claims verification, and subsidy reimbursement settlement.
* **Transition Cut-off**: Supplementary/additional claims from lending banks for the **January–March 2026 quarter** continue to be processed by RBI; all claims from **April 1, 2026 onward** are handled by EXIM Bank.
* **Credit Norms**: Export credit continues to be sanctioned and disbursed by scheduled commercial banks adhering to RBI's consolidated directions on export credit facilities.
* **Institutional Background**: EXIM Bank was established by an Act of Parliament in **September 1981** (commenced operations in **1982**), headquartered in **Mumbai**, and is wholly owned by the Government of India (MD: Harsha Bangari). It acts as the backbone for the government's *Niryat Protsahan* export initiative.

**Know / Understand**
* Relieving the RBI from day-to-day administrative processing of commercial interest subvention aligns with international central banking norms, shifting operational subsidy management to specialized development finance institutions (DFIs).

### 2. MoSPI Adopts Producer Price Index (PPI) & Double Deflation Method for GDP Estimation
* **Priority:** P1 — Critical / Deep | **Revision Effort:** ~8 min | **Category:** Macro Economy & National Accounts
* **Source:** Smartkeeda (Aug 2026) | **Origin:** Ministry of Statistics & Programme Implementation

**What Happened**
* In one of the most significant national accounting methodological overhauls in decades, MoSPI announced that India will transition from the Wholesale Price Index (WPI) to the output **Producer Price Index (PPI)** for deflating Gross Domestic Product (GDP) and Gross Value Added (GVA) data.

**Must Memorize (Methodological Reforms)**
* **Double Deflation Method**: Replaces the single deflation method. Under Double Deflation:
  * **Output values** are deflated using the **Output PPI**.
  * **Intermediate input costs** are separately deflated using relevant **Input Price Indices**.
  * The difference yields a highly accurate estimate of **Real Gross Value Added (GVA)**, preventing distorted GDP figures caused by diverging input costs and output prices.
* **WPI Limitations**: Existing WPI series (base year 2011–12, tracking 697 items across Primary Articles, Fuel & Power, and Manufactured Products) excludes the services sector entirely, despite services contributing $>50%$ of India's GDP. PPI captures both goods and services prices before reaching consumers.
* **Complementary GDP Reforms**:
  * Base year updated to **2022–23** (from 2011–12).
  * Introduction of India's 1st trial **Index of Services Production (ISP)** in July 2026.

### 3. Taxation and Other Laws (Amendment) Bill, 2026: Enabling Provision for UPI MDR Charges
* **Priority:** P1 — Critical / Deep | **Revision Effort:** ~8 min | **Category:** Digital Payments & Tax Law
* **Source:** Smartkeeda (Aug 2026) | **Origin:** Lok Sabha Bill Passage

**What Happened**
* The Lok Sabha passed the *Taxation and Other Laws (Amendment) Bill, 2026*, amending Section 10A of the *Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007* (PSSA) to empower the Central Government to permit banks and payment service providers to levy charges/Merchant Discount Rate (MDR) on specified electronic payment modes including UPI.

**Must Memorize (Statutory Amendments & UPI Scale)**
* **Section 10A PSSA Overhaul**: Previously, Section 10A barred banks and payment aggregators from charging any fee on digital payment modes mandated under Section 269SU of the Income-tax Act (RuPay debit cards, BHIM-UPI QR codes for businesses with annual turnover $>₹50	ext{ crore}$). The amendment removes this statutory blanket restriction, allowing government-notified charges.
* **No Immediate Levy**: The legislation is an **enabling framework**; charges will only apply if and when the Ministry of Finance issues a specific gazette notification setting rate caps and turnover thresholds.
* **Associated Acts Amended**: Also amends the *Income Tax Act, 2025* and *Finance Act, 2026*.
* **UPI Monthly Milestone**: Recorded **23.66 billion transactions** worth **₹29.88 lakh crore** in July 2026.

### 4. CBDC-Based Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) Launched under PMGKAY in Chandigarh & DNH
* **Priority:** P1 — Critical / Deep | **Revision Effort:** ~7 min | **Category:** Digital Payments & CBDC
* **Source:** Smartkeeda (Aug 2026) | **Origin:** Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

**What Happened**
* The Government of India launched the nation's 1st large-scale application of Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC) in a welfare programme, rolling out **CBDC-based DBT under PMGKAY** (*Pradhan Mantri Garib Kalyan Anna Yojana*) in the Union Territories of **Chandigarh** and **Dadra & Nagar Haveli**.

**Must Memorize (CBDC Architecture)**
* **Mechanism**: Beneficiaries receive food subsidies directly into their RBI Digital Rupee (e₹) wallets as **programmable tokens** instead of standard bank deposits.
* **Programmability**: Tokens can only be spent at empanelled Fair Price Shops (FPS) / grain merchants, ensuring 100% end-use verification and eliminating leakage.
* **First UT Adopters**: Chandigarh and Dadra & Nagar Haveli became the **1st Union Territories** to achieve complete digital adoption of CBDC food subsidies (building on earlier localized pilots in Puducherry and Gujarat).

### 5. SEBI Proposes Colour-Coded "Credit Risk-o-Meter" for Debt Securities
* **Priority:** P1 — Critical / Deep | **Revision Effort:** ~7 min | **Category:** Capital Markets & SEBI
* **Source:** Smartkeeda (Aug 2026) | **Origin:** SEBI Consultation Paper

**What Happened**
* SEBI issued a consultation paper proposing a mandatory visual, colour-coded **Credit Risk-o-Meter** for corporate bonds and debt securities to assist retail investors in assessing credit default risk.

**Must Memorize (Risk Scale & Regulatory Rules)**
* **Visual Scale**: Maps credit ratings from **AAA to D** across **6 standardized colour-coded risk tiers** (ranging from *Lowest Credit Risk* for AAA to *High to Very High Risk of Default* for B+, B, B-, C+, C, C-, and D).
* **Mandatory Platforms**: Must be displayed in offer documents, abridged prospectuses, private placement memorandums, advertisements, and on **Online Bond Platform Providers (OBPPs)** websites and apps.
* **Conservative Rule**: Where a debt instrument is rated by multiple Credit Rating Agencies (CRAs), the Credit Risk-o-Meter must reflect the **lowest rating**.
* **Unsecured Debt Highlight**: Unsecured bonds must display the word **'unsecured' in bold red text**. OBPPs must update the meter in real-time upon any rating migration.

### 6. SEBI Draft Settlement Regulations 2026: Fast-Track Route for Cases up to ₹10 Lakh
* **Priority:** P1 — Critical / Memorize | **Revision Effort:** ~6 min | **Category:** Capital Markets & Enforcement
* **Source:** Smartkeeda (Aug 2026) | **Origin:** SEBI Consultation Paper

**What Happened**
* SEBI published draft *SEBI (Settlement of Proceedings) Regulations, 2026* (reviewing 2018 regulations) to streamline consent settlements without admission or denial of guilt.

**Must Memorize (Key Procedural Reforms)**
* **Fast-Track Settlement**: For cases involving amounts **up to ₹10 lakh**, applications bypass the High Powered Advisory Committee (HPAC) entirely, moving directly from the Internal Committee to a panel of Whole Time Members (WTMs).
* **Fee Rationalization**: Scraps the additional **20% settlement surcharge** on multiple-proceeding cases; reduces refiling charges for withdrawn applications.
* **Appellate Stage Settlement**: For the first time, permits entities to file for settlement even during appeals before the **Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT)** and Supreme Court.
* **Enforcement Backlog Context**: During FY26, SEBI received 439 applications, disposed 170, and had 499 applications pending at year-end.

### 7. CBDT Operationalises Foreign Assets of Small Taxpayers-Disclosure Scheme (FAST-DS), 2026
* **Priority:** P1 — Critical / Memorize | **Revision Effort:** ~6 min | **Category:** Direct Taxes & Compliance
* **Source:** Smartkeeda (Aug 2026) | **Origin:** Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT)

**What Happened**
* CBDT operationalised the *Foreign Assets of Small Taxpayers-Disclosure Scheme (FAST-DS), 2026*, notified under Chapter IV (Sections 130–144) of the *Finance Act, 2026*.

**Must Memorize (Scheme Timeline & Scope)**
* **One-Time Window**: Open from **August 16, 2026 to December 31, 2026**.
* **Valuation Benchmark Date**: **March 31, 2026**.
* **Covered Assets (4 Categories)**:
  1. Undisclosed foreign assets/financial interests abroad.
  2. Undisclosed foreign income chargeable to Indian tax.
  3. Assets acquired while non-resident but omitted upon returning to resident status.
  4. Assets acquired from taxed income but omitted from the Foreign Assets (FA) Schedule in ITR.
* **Filing & Payment**: Electronic filing in **Form 1**; Principal DG/DG Income Tax (Systems) issues determination order in **Form 2**; declarant gets **2 months** to pay determined amount without interest, gaining immunity from prosecution.
